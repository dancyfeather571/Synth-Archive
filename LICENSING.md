Everything committed to this repository (README, docs, scripts) is original work, released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## 2. Sample content: not open-licensed

The sampled expansion (sample monoliths, sample audio, sample maps, presets, artwork) is **not** covered by the MIT License and is **not** released under MIT, GPL, Creative Commons, CC0, or the public domain.

- Much of the source audio comes from [Legowelt's free sample library](https://www.legowelt.org/samples/). Legowelt permits using those samples in music productions. Redistributing them inside another sampled instrument is not explicitly authorized.
- Resampling, layering, processing, or blending a recording does not remove the original recording's copyright. Derived audio is treated as containing the original.
- Some audio comes from sources whose terms have not yet been confirmed. See [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

For these reasons the sampled expansion is **not distributed** here. It will only be published, free of charge, once redistribution permission is confirmed in writing for every source it contains. The release notes will then state the exact terms that apply to the audio.

- Use, modify, and share the code and documentation under MIT.
